# 猫语匣（CatSpeak）隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月6日

猫语匣是一款在设备上使用的猫咪互动应用，用于播放猫叫、记录日常、做性格小测试，以及显示主屏幕小组件。我们没有自己的服务器，也不要求注册账号。

## 我们保存的资料

这些内容保存在你的 iPhone 上，包括：

- 你填写的猫名、性别、是否绝育、生日和体重
- 驱虫、疫苗日期，以及喂食、铲屎、换水、玩耍等打卡
- 心情备注、花销记录、每日猫签和性格测试结果
- 提醒开关与时间、首次引导是否完成
- 小组件需要的今日摘要（通过本机 App Group 交给小组件，不上传给我们）

如果这台设备登录了 iCloud，上述资料会通过 Apple 的 iCloud 键值存储在你自己的设备之间同步。同步由 Apple 处理，我们看不到、也拿不到这些内容。

## 我们不收集的内容

我们不收集姓名、电话、精确位置、通讯录、照片、麦克风或相机数据。应用不会录音，也不会把你输入的句子发到网上翻译。我们不接入广告或第三方统计，不出售资料，也不用这些资料做用户画像。

## 通知

只有在你允许通知后，应用才会在本机安排喂食、铲屎、换水、玩耍、晚间回访，以及驱虫、疫苗提醒。通知内容留在设备上。可在系统设置里随时关闭。

## 儿童

猫语匣面向照顾猫咪的成年人。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

## 医疗说明

驱虫和疫苗日期只是习惯提醒，不是诊断、处方或兽医建议。

## 保留与删除

卸载应用会删除这台设备上的本地资料。已同步到 iCloud 的键值可能仍留在你的 iCloud 中，可在系统的 iCloud 设置里管理。我们没有账号可注销，因为根本没有账号。

## 联系

隐私问题请发邮件至：[1175903571@qq.com](mailto:1175903571@qq.com)。

# CatSpeak Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

CatSpeak is an on-device app for playing cat sounds, logging daily care, taking a short personality quiz, and showing a Home Screen widget. We do not run our own server, and the app does not require an account.

## Information stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone:

- The cat’s name, sex, neuter status, birthday, and weight, if you enter them
- Deworming and vaccine dates, and check-ins for feeding, scooping, water, and play
- Mood notes, spending records, the daily note, and the personality quiz result
- Reminder switches and times, and whether first-run setup is finished
- A short summary for the widget, shared on the device through an App Group and not uploaded to us

If the device is signed in to iCloud, this information can sync between your own devices through Apple’s iCloud Key-Value Store. Apple handles that sync. We cannot see or receive it.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data. The app does not record audio, and sentences you type are not sent online for translation. We do not use ads or third-party analytics, we do not sell information, and we do not build advertising profiles.

## Notifications

Only after you allow notifications does the app schedule local reminders for feeding, scooping, water, play, an evening check-in, and deworming or vaccine dates. Those notifications stay on the device. You can turn them off in Settings.

## Children

CatSpeak is meant for adults who care for a cat. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Not medical advice

Deworming and vaccine dates are habit reminders only. They are not a diagnosis, a prescription, or veterinary advice.

## Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes the local copy on that device. Values already synced to iCloud may remain in your iCloud account until you manage them in iCloud settings. There is no account to delete, because the app has none.
